# D086 architectural references

Consulted2026-10-25. These are original small procedural abstractions, not measured replicas. No photographs, external meshes or textures enter the product. Recorded features and original design choices are deliberately separated.

- **Iron borough market:** [Historic England, Burton market1477327](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1477327), iron supports and partly glazed roofing. The segmental hall, aisle proportions and vestibule are original.
- **Raised guild market:** [Minchinhampton market house1340484](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1340484), raised upper rooms/open market arrangement, plus [Historic England's English market-hall survey](https://heritagecalling.com/2024/03/14/the-timeless-charm-of-english-market-towns-and-halls/) for timber market vocabulary. The composite stair and timber hall are not a claim about one exact building.
- **Butter-market colonnade:** [Coflein23176](https://coflein.gov.uk/en/sites/23176), an open stone-column market with hipped roof. The simplified paired supports and entry pediment are original.
- **Wool exchange:** [Bradford HER MWY5896](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/heritage-gateway/results/record/c08bded6-08f6-4d13-8699-29c79effb01d/MWY5896), triangular site, Gothic trading hall and clock tower. The mesh plan and spire proportions are original.
- **Customs trading house:** [Historic England, Poole custom house1275358](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1275358), a Georgian customs/commercial building. Our U-court, unequal wings and gateway are an original composition, not attributed to Poole. Gameplay remains tradepost, not a new tax-collection simulation.
- **Free library:** [Herne Hill library1185521](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1185521), public-library fenestration and radial book arrangement. The octagonal reading rotunda and splayed wings are original, not the listed library's recorded massing.
- **Sorting post office:** [North Shields municipal complex1299975](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1299975), civic/postal functions and Dutch-gable vocabulary. Dutch gables belong to another component of that complex; this is an explicitly original composite, not an assertion that the source post office has these exact gables.
- **Board school:** [Harrington school1390509](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1390509), E-plan classroom organization. The reference is1875 Victorian; our original design uses later Edwardian civic vocabulary and does not redate the source.
- **Fire station:** [Accrington fire station1072755](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1072755), interwar stepped/flat-roof station and hose tower. The source has five garage openings; our three-bay brick version is an original adaptation.
- **Cottage clinic:** [Woburn cottage hospital1390557](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1390557), Arts-and-Crafts hipped roofs, timber verandas and sunrooms. Our splayed wards/courtyard composition is original and gameplay remains the established clinic service.
