## Frozen acceptance

No new IDs, no invented variants, no external assets, no simulation/history/save/tool edits. Retain all 130 prior models and all non-target raw geometry and ordered trees. Respect each inherited height, parcel, construction behavior and weighted geometry ceiling. Architecture belongs in W/O; decorative paving never supplies structural uniqueness. Explicit complete walls and roofs, correct normals and winding, no degenerate triangles. A structure must differ by topology, not only palette, scale, translation, rotation, triangulation or detail.

Add new gates rather than weaken or skip old ones.

Studio renders are not production gallery or physical-phone evidence. Review source/geometry hashes accompany screenshots. Full latest-head formal CI precedes merge eligibility.
